## Privacy restructure

The repository is public, so the earlier build's bundled task audit (private task excerpts, evaluator workbook cells, grader text) and the model-run receipt were removed from the tree and the bundle. They live under `private/` (ignored by git), load through *Load private evidence module*, and the audit views render from the module's own projects and rows. The illustrations were replaced with synthetic cases, and the hard-coded task figures in the earlier UI, docs, and tests were removed. The validation-time lint confirmed the shipped HTML references neither module.

## Boundaries

- Structural checks, lint, and the shortlist ranking are deterministic; wording cues are explicitly heuristic. None of them judges financial semantics or predicts a score.
- XLSX extraction reads formulas and saved values; it does not recalculate. DOCX anchors are paragraph and table indices; PDF text is not extracted.
- The guidance draft is a template over the author's records; it must be rewritten in the author's own words before use.
- No model was called. The archived local companion needs the installed runtime, a private module, and fresh authorization; the delivered one-run permission is consumed.
- The synthetic illustrations, library records, and QA fixtures are invented. No task answer, research finding, or observed run is represented.
